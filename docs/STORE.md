```
ЧЕТЫРЕ ПОЯСА, ВОСЕМЬ СТРАЖЕЙ
```

### Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Sixteen floors down into Nav — the underworld of Slavic folk tales. Death is
final here: saving is a pause, not a spare life.

FOUR BELTS, EIGHT GUARDIANS

The descent is cut into four parts of four floors, and each is its own world.
The Boneyard: dry crypts and corridors. The Black Mire: caves, water nearly
everywhere, no doors at all. Koschei's Kingdom: cold halls with chasms
underfoot. The Scorch: hot stone and burning rivers.

Every belt holds two: a lesser guardian halfway down and its master at the
bottom. Mara casts delusion. Viy keeps his eyelids down for a few turns, then
lifts them and strikes everything he can see. Baba Yaga hides behind her huts
while they stand. Koschei rises again while the needle survives somewhere on the
floor. Zmey Gorynych breathes fire more often the fewer heads he has left.

Not one of them is merely a large health bar. Each fights on a mechanic, and
every mechanic has an answer you can work out. Viy telegraphs the gaze a turn
early. The fall of the last hut is announced. Koschei's first resurrection
reveals the floor, so there is somewhere to look for the needle.

ONE SEED, ONE DUNGEON

Every floor is generated from a seed. The same seed always yields the same
floor, the same creatures and the same items — on any device. Anything you find
can be handed to someone else as one line of text.

There is a daily seed too: the same one for everybody, on a shared score table.

SIX HEROES

Vityaz, Vedun, Tat, Znahar, Kuznets and Bogatyr. Three differ in numbers, three
in behaviour: the herbalist knows every potion and brews them stronger, the
smith finds gear one grade better, and the champion's blow reaches every
adjacent enemy at once.

WHAT ELSE IS IN IT

• Twelve pieces of gear that carry a mechanic rather than a number: a club that
  knocks down, a boar spear for the big ones, a shirt that turns one blow aside
  per floor, charms that hold off poison and fire. Four sets of three — a
  completed set takes the hero's whole kit.
• A bestiary: every creature you meet stays in the list with its description.
• Achievements that persist between runs.
• A post-mortem: the last blows, what finished you, and the healing potion still
  in your pack.
• Several endings — the victory epilogue depends on how the run went.
• A theme for each guardian, changing with the phases of the fight.
• A colour-blind palette.
• Russian and English, switchable at any moment.

PLAINLY, WHAT IS NOT HERE

No advertising. No in-app purchases. No data collection of any kind. No account,
no sign-in. The game never touches the network and runs entirely offline.

The source is open under the MIT licence:
github.com/TheToshix/nav-roguelike
```

## Что ещё спросит Play

**Категория.** Games → Role Playing (или Strategy — роглайк формально подходит под обе; RPG
ближе).

**Теги.** До пяти: roguelike, dungeon crawler, turn-based, pixel art, offline.

**Возрастной рейтинг.** Отвечайте по анкете честно. Бои есть, крови и расчленёнки нет,
азартных игр нет, покупок нет. Ожидаемо выйдет невысокий рейтинг.

**Контакт для поддержки.** Play требует email. Либо ваш, либо заведите отдельный —
он будет виден всем на странице приложения.

**Страна и цена.** Бесплатно. Если поставите цену, потребуется настроить платёжный профиль.
